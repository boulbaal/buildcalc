    How it works

    The calculator divides the total rise by the code maximum riser height of 7.75 in (19.7 cm) and rounds up to get the number of risers. Dividing the total rise by that count gives the exact riser height to lay out on the stringer:

    risers = ceil(total rise / 7.75)
    riser height = total rise / risers
    treads = risers - 1
    total run = treads × tread depth
    comfort = 2 × riser height + tread depth  (aim for 24-25 in / 61-63.5 cm)

    Your target riser height is shown next to the actual one so you can see how close the layout comes. Code limits here follow the US IRC (max riser 7.75 in, min tread 10 in); local codes can differ, always verify before you cut.

    Worked example

    Say the total rise from floor to floor is 100 inches. Then 100 / 7.75 = 12.9, rounded up to 13 risers. Each riser is 100 / 13 = 7.69 in, and there are 13 - 1 = 12 treads. With a 10.5 in tread depth the total run is 12 × 10.5 = 126 in. The comfort value is 2 × 7.69 + 10.5 = 25.88 in, slightly above the ideal 24-25 in range, so a deeper tread or an extra riser could feel better underfoot.

    FAQ

    What is the maximum riser height by code?

    The US IRC allows a maximum riser height of 7.75 inches (about 19.7 cm) for residential stairs. Risers within one flight must not vary by more than 3/8 inch. Commercial codes and many local codes are stricter, so always verify with your local building department.

    What is the ideal riser height for stairs?

    Most people find risers of 7 to 7.5 inches (18 to 19 cm) the most comfortable to climb. Shallower risers around 6 inches suit outdoor steps and older users. Anything above the 7.75 inch IRC maximum feels steep and is not allowed on new residential stairs.

    What is the 2R+T rule for stairs?

    The 2R+T comfort rule says twice the riser height plus the tread depth should land between 24 and 25 inches (61 to 63.5 cm). It approximates a natural walking stride. Stairs inside that range feel comfortable; stairs outside it feel too steep or too shallow.

    How do I calculate the number of steps in a staircase?

    Measure the total rise from finished floor to finished floor, then divide by the maximum riser height of 7.75 inches and round up. That gives the riser count; dividing the total rise by it gives the exact riser height. The number of treads is one less than the number of risers.

    What is the minimum tread depth by code?

    The US IRC requires a minimum tread depth of 10 inches (about 25.4 cm), measured nose to nose. Deeper treads of 10.5 to 11 inches are more comfortable and common. Treads within a flight must not vary by more than 3/8 inch. Check local amendments before building.
