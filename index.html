Why this site exists

Most calculator sites bury a simple formula under ads, popups and newsletter walls. These five tools do the opposite: the calculator is at the top, the formula is printed under the result, and the page loads in a fraction of a second. If a tool saves you a wasted trip to the store, there is a donation button after your result. That is the whole business model.

All results are estimates for planning purposes and not a substitute for professional advice. Always check local building codes and manufacturer instructions.
