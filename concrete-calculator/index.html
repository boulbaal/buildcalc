    How it works

    A slab is a simple box: length times width times thickness gives the volume. The calculator converts thickness to the same unit as the sides, adds your waste margin, then divides by the yield of one bag and rounds up to whole bags:

    volume (cu ft) = length (ft) × width (ft) × thickness (in) / 12
    cubic yards = volume / 27
    bags = ceil(volume × (1 + waste / 100) / bag yield)

    Bag yields: an 80 lb bag makes about 0.60 cu ft, a 60 lb bag 0.45 cu ft, a 40 lb bag 0.30 cu ft, and a 25 kg bag about 0.0123 m³.

    Worked example

    Say you pour a 10 × 10 ft patio, 4 inches thick. The volume is 10 × 10 × 4/12 = 33.33 cu ft, which is 33.33 / 27 = 1.23 cu yd. With 80 lb bags at 0.60 cu ft each, that is 33.33 / 0.60 = 55.6, rounded up to 56 bags. Add the standard 10% waste margin and you should buy 62 bags. Bags come back easier than a second store run, so round up.

    FAQ

    How many 80 lb bags of concrete are in a cubic yard?

    An 80 lb bag yields about 0.60 cubic feet of concrete, and a cubic yard is 27 cubic feet. So you need 27 / 0.60 = 45 bags of 80 lb concrete mix per cubic yard. For 60 lb bags it is 60, and for 40 lb bags it is 90.

    How thick should a concrete slab be?

    4 inches is the typical thickness for patios, walkways and shed slabs. Driveways that carry cars usually need 5 to 6 inches, and slabs for heavy vehicles or workshops often go to 6 inches or more. Check your local building code before you pour.

    Do I need rebar or wire mesh in a slab?

    Small slabs like walkways often do fine with just control joints, but wire mesh or rebar helps hold cracks tight in patios, driveways and garage floors. Place reinforcement in the middle of the slab thickness, not on the ground, so it can actually do its job.

    When should I order a ready-mix truck instead of bags?

    Around 1 cubic yard is the usual tipping point. That is already 45 bags of 80 lb mix to haul and mix by hand. Above a yard, a ready-mix truck is faster, more consistent and often cheaper per yard, though many suppliers charge a short-load fee for small orders.

    How long does concrete take to cure?

    Concrete sets in hours but cures slowly. You can usually walk on it after 24 to 48 hours and drive on it after about 7 days. It reaches most of its design strength at 28 days. Keep it damp for the first week so it cures strong instead of drying out.
