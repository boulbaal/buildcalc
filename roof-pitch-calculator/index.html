    How it works

    Roof pitch is the ratio between vertical rise and horizontal run. This calculator converts between the three common ways of expressing it, plus the pitch multiplier used for area estimates:

    angle = atan(rise / run)
    percent = rise / run × 100
    pitch = (rise / run × 12) : 12
    multiplier = √(1 + (rise / run)²)

    Worked example

    Say your roof rises 6 inches for every 12 inches of run. Then rise / run = 0.5, so the angle is atan(0.5) = 26.57°, the slope is 50%, and the pitch is written 6:12. The multiplier is √(1 + 0.25) = 1.118: a house with a 1,000 sq ft footprint under that roof has roughly 1,118 sq ft of actual roof surface.

    FAQ

    What is a 6/12 roof pitch in degrees?

    A 6/12 pitch means the roof rises 6 inches for every 12 inches of horizontal run. That equals 26.57 degrees, or a 50% slope. It is one of the most common pitches on American homes.

    What is the pitch multiplier used for?

    It converts the flat footprint area of a roof into the true sloped surface area. Multiply the footprint by the multiplier to estimate shingles or underlayment. For a 6/12 pitch the multiplier is 1.118.

    What is the minimum pitch for shingles?

    Most asphalt shingle manufacturers require at least a 2/12 pitch, and below 4/12 they require extra underlayment. Below 2/12 you need a membrane roof instead. Always check the manufacturer's instructions.

    How do I measure roof pitch from inside the attic?

    Hold a level horizontally against a rafter, mark 12 inches from one end, then measure vertically from that mark up to the rafter. That vertical distance in inches is your pitch in X:12 notation.

    Is a steeper roof pitch better?

    Steeper roofs shed water and snow faster and often last longer, but they cost more to build and are more dangerous to work on. Common residential pitches range from 4/12 to 9/12.
